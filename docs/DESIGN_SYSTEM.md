# ORBIT — design language V5

**Single memorable signature:** a living radial constellation surrounded by a user-arranged orbit of translucent glass-like objects, on an animated colorful mist; an editorial paper journal is a deliberately different tactile material.

- Background light periwinkle/blush/cyan/ochre gradients, slowly drifting independent of text; explicit reduced-motion support. No high-frequency particles or flickering.
- Spatial objects: translucent white and blue-white glass, inset top highlights, subtle cool drop shadows, visually separated without a conventional grid. High-contrast text prioritized over exact proprietary Apple Liquid Glass replication.
- Constellation: visually unboxed; faint orbit rings; branch nodes glass; task stacks colorful unboxed text.
- Journal: restrained cream paper with softly ruled lines and book spine; readable Georgia serif for authored text, system sans-serif for controls. The quill stays decorative; no handwriting simulation.
- Screen controls: global clock/brand overlay top; spatial minimap with dots; three board camera controls; node-specific utilities and captures stay connected to their widgets. Avoid transient menus that cover the map.
- Motion: background blur drifts very slowly; node elasticity; camera glide; every transition cancelable via direct interaction. Disable ambient on reduced motion.
- Mobile: 320px+, thumb-friendly focus transitions, nav mini-map placed away from the capture bar; no desktop hover requirement for task detail or journal.
- Accessibility trade-off: Apple's HIG recommends Liquid Glass mainly for functional overlays; user prefers glass surfaces as content. Treat those as *custom glass-styled web surfaces*; test legibility, contrast and reduce-transparency alternatives before production.
